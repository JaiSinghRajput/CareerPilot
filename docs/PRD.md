# CareerPilot AI — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Vision
CareerPilot AI is an AI-powered career platform that helps students and professionals create ATS-friendly resumes, discover projects, find jobs and internships, prepare for interviews, and improve employability through personalized AI guidance.

## Problem Statement
Job seekers struggle with:
- Creating professional ATS-friendly resumes.
- Identifying portfolio-worthy projects.
- Finding relevant jobs and internships.
- Matching resumes against job descriptions.
- Preparing for interviews.

CareerPilot AI solves these problems with AI-first workflows.

## Target Users
- College Students
- Freshers
- Experienced Professionals
- Career Switchers
- Internship Seekers
